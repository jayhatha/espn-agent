  <action>
Create `scenario-edge-cases.test.ts` testing engine behavior under specific auction conditions. Each test constructs a scenario state and verifies engine output.

Use helpers to create mock `DraftPick[]`, `AuctionValue[]`, and `PoolPlayer[]` arrays for each scenario.

**Scenario 1: Early value run (bargain buying)**
Setup: 10-team draft, first 20 picks all at 120%+ of model value (league is overspending). Remaining pool has players whose model value exceeds their market price.
Test: Call `computeInflation` with these picks. Verify `inflationFactor > 1.05` (buying window or strong value). Verify `inflationBand` is "buying_window" or "strong_value". This confirms the engine would recommend aggressive bidding during a value run.

**Scenario 2: Late auction inflation**
Setup: Draft is 70% complete. Most high-value players are gone. Remaining budget across league exceeds remaining player value.
Test: Call `computeInflation`. Verify `inflationFactor > 1.10`. Call `computeBudgetCurve` with remaining budget and spots. Verify `maxBidRecommendation` is appropriately elevated (inflation-adjusted bid > raw dollar value).

**Scenario 3: Position scarcity**
Setup: Pool of 20 players. Only 2 catchers remain. 8 teams still need a catcher. Create roster slots with C:1.
Test: Call `applyScarcityAdjustment` with baseSGP=3.0, position="C", remainingSlots=8, remainingPlayers=2. Verify the adjusted SGP is significantly higher than base (scarcityFactor = 8/2 = 4.0, multiplier = 1.5). Exact: `3.0 * (1 + (4.0 - 1) * 1.5) = 3.0 * 5.5 = 16.5`.

**Scenario 4: Endgame mode**
Setup: Budget = $18, remaining roster spots = 8.
Test: Call `computeBudgetCurve` with budget=18, remainingRosterSpots=8, totalBudget=260, totalRosterSize=22, inflationFactor=1.1, currentPlayerValue=5. Verify:
  - `endgameMode === true`
  - `averageRemainingPerPlayer` is low (usable = 18 - 7 = 11, avg = 11/8 = 1.375)
  - `maxBidRecommendation` is small (capped by usable budget constraints)

**Scenario 5: Budget pacing — ahead of schedule**
Setup: Halfway through draft (11 of 22 spots filled), budget=$180 (spent only $80 of $260).
Test: `computeBudgetCurve` with remainingBudget=180, remainingRosterSpots=11. Verify `pacingStatus === "ahead"` and `budgetPressure > 1.05`.

**Scenario 6: Budget pacing — behind schedule**
Setup: Halfway through draft, budget=$90 (spent $170 of $260, too aggressive).
Test: `computeBudgetCurve` with remainingBudget=90, remainingRosterSpots=11. Verify `pacingStatus === "behind"` and `budgetPressure < 0.95`.

**Scenario 7: SP tier cap progression in roster building**
Setup: Create pool with 10 SPs at various prices ($25, $18, $15, $12, $10, $8, $5, $3, $2, $1). Roster with 6 P slots. Budget=$60.
Test: Call `optimizeRoster`. Verify the selected pitchers respect tier caps: first SP can be up to $18, second up to $12, etc. Verify no SP in roster exceeds its tier cap based on draft order.

**Scenario 8: Mixed hitter/pitcher budget allocation**
Setup: Pool with both hitters and pitchers. Budget=$260. Full roster slots.
Test: Call `computeBudgetCurve` passing hitterSpent=100, pitcherSpent=30. Verify the returned signal includes correct hitterSpent and pitcherSpent values and budget targets.
